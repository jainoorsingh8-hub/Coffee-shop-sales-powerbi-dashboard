# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard — Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The main objective of this project is to transform raw coffee shop transaction data into meaningful and actionable business insights through interactive dashboards and data visualizations.

The dashboard analyzes coffee shop sales based on **revenue, number of orders, coffee products, payment methods, time of day, weekdays, hours, and monthly trends**.

The project uses **DAX measures and calculated columns** to create dynamic KPIs and analytical metrics. Interactive slicers allow users to filter the dashboard according to different years, months, coffee products, payment methods, and time periods.

The dashboard is divided into **three analytical pages**, with each page focusing on a different aspect of the business.

---

# 📊 Page 1 — Executive Overview

The **Executive Overview** page provides a high-level summary of the overall performance of the coffee shop.

This page is designed for managers and decision-makers who want to understand the business performance quickly without going through individual transactions.

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators

The top section contains important KPIs such as:

* **Total Sales** — Total revenue generated from all transactions.
* **Total Orders** — Total number of transactions recorded.
* **Coffee Types** — Number of unique coffee products available.
* **Average Order Value** — Average amount spent per transaction.
* **Average Daily Sales** — Average revenue generated per day.
* **Maximum Sale** — Highest individual transaction value.

These KPIs provide an immediate snapshot of the coffee shop's financial and operational performance.

## 📈 Sales Trend Analysis

A **Monthly Sales Trend** visualization is used to analyze how sales change over time.

This allows users to identify:

* Increasing or decreasing sales trends
* High-performing months
* Low-performing months
* Seasonal changes in sales

## ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis

The **Sales by Coffee** visualization compares revenue generated by different coffee products.

This helps identify:

* Best-performing coffee products
* Products generating the highest revenue
* Products that may require additional marketing
* Differences in product popularity

## 💳 Payment Method Analysis

A **Payment Method** visualization compares sales generated through different payment methods such as:

* Cash
* Card

This helps understand customer payment preferences and the contribution of each payment method to total revenue.

## 🌞 Time of Day Analysis

The dashboard also analyzes orders according to different periods of the day.

This helps determine whether the coffee shop receives more business during:

* Morning
* Afternoon
* Evening
* Night

## 📅 Weekday Sales Analysis

Sales are analyzed across different days of the week to identify the strongest and weakest business days.

This can help the business with:

* Staff scheduling
* Inventory planning
* Promotional campaigns
* Resource allocation

## 🎯 Sales Performance vs Target

A **Gauge Chart** is included to compare actual sales performance against a defined sales target.

This provides a quick visual indication of whether the business is:

* Below target
* Approaching target
* Meeting target
* Exceeding target

---

# ☕ Page 2 — Product Analysis

The **Product Analysis** page focuses specifically on coffee products and their sales performance.

The purpose of this page is to understand which products are contributing most to the business revenue and which products are most frequently purchased.

## 🎯 Product KPIs

The page includes KPIs such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Coffee Types
* Average Order Value

These metrics provide a summary of overall product performance.

## 🏆 Top 10 Coffee Products

A **Top 10 Coffee Products** bar chart identifies the coffee products generating the highest sales.

This visualization allows users to quickly identify the most valuable products in the business.

The information can be used to:

* Focus marketing efforts on popular products
* Maintain sufficient inventory
* Identify customer preferences
* Improve product strategies

## 🛒 Orders by Coffee

The **Orders by Coffee** visualization compares the number of orders received for each coffee product.

Revenue alone does not always indicate popularity, so comparing order volume provides an additional perspective.

For example, a product may have:

* High revenue with fewer orders because of a higher price
* Many orders but lower revenue because of a lower price

## 🥧 Revenue Contribution by Coffee

A revenue contribution visualization shows how individual coffee products contribute to the overall sales.

This helps identify products that account for a significant percentage of total revenue.

## 💰 Average Price by Coffee

The **Average Price by Coffee** visualization compares the average transaction value of different coffee products.

This allows the business to understand the pricing structure across products.

## 🏷️ Price Category Analysis

Coffee products are categorized into different price groups:

* Low
* Medium
* High

The dashboard analyzes the number of orders associated with each price category.

This helps identify whether customers generally prefer lower-priced, medium-priced, or higher-priced products.

## 💳 Payment Method by Coffee

A stacked visualization compares payment methods across different coffee products.

This helps determine whether customers purchasing specific products are more likely to use:

* Cash
* Card

## 🏆 Top Coffee Ranking Over Time

A **Ribbon Chart** is used to analyze how coffee product rankings change over different months.

This is useful for identifying:

* Products that consistently remain popular
* Products whose rankings increase
* Products whose rankings decrease
* Changes in customer preferences over time

This provides a more detailed view of product performance than a simple bar chart.

## 📋 Product Details

A product details table provides a more detailed view containing information such as:

* Coffee Name
* Total Orders
* Total Sales
* Average Price
* Sales Contribution %

This allows users to investigate individual products in more detail.

---

# ⏰ Page 3 — Time Analysis

The **Time Analysis** page focuses on understanding when customers make purchases and when the coffee shop generates the highest revenue.

This page helps answer important business questions such as:

> What is the busiest hour?

> Which day generates the most sales?

> What time of day generates the highest revenue?

> How do sales change from month to month?

## 🎯 Time-Based KPIs

The page includes:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Average Daily Sales
* Orders per Day
* Peak Sales Hour

The **Peak Sales Hour** KPI identifies the hour during which the coffee shop generates the highest sales.

## ⏰ Sales by Hour

A line chart analyzes sales across different hours of the day.

This helps identify:

* Peak business hours
* Low-demand periods
* Potential staffing requirements
* Suitable times for promotions

Understanding hourly demand can help the business optimize its operations.

## 🌞 Sales by Time of Day

Sales are grouped into different periods such as:

* Morning
* Afternoon
* Evening
* Night

This makes it easier to identify the most profitable period of the day.

## 📅 Orders by Weekday

The dashboard compares the number of orders received on different days of the week.

This can help the business understand weekly customer behavior and plan resources accordingly.

## 🔥 Sales Heatmap

A **Weekday × Time of Day heatmap** is used to provide a combined analysis of when sales are highest.

The heatmap makes it easy to identify combinations such as:

* Monday Morning
* Friday Evening
* Saturday Afternoon
* Sunday Morning

The intensity of the cells represents the sales generated during each period.

This is particularly useful for identifying the busiest periods of the business.

## 📈 Monthly Sales Trend

A monthly sales trend visualization shows how revenue changes throughout the dataset's time period.

It helps identify:

* Growth patterns
* Declining periods
* Seasonal fluctuations
* High-performing months

## 💳 Payment Method by Time

A stacked column visualization compares cash and card transactions across different times of the day.

This provides insight into customer payment behavior and can help the business understand how payment preferences change throughout the day.
